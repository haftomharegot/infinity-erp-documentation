# Feature Overview - Product Price Updates

## 1. Overview

Product Price Updates is an inventory management workflow that enables authorized users to update the sales prices and costs of multiple products through a single, controlled process.

Instead of opening and editing each product individually, users can define a price update batch, select the products to be affected, configure the desired changes, and review the proposed results before applying them.

The workflow also maintains product-level price history, allowing users to review previous and updated prices, track changes in product costs, and identify the outcomes of individual updates.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of Product Price Updates is to simplify and control bulk product price changes while maintaining a record of the changes made.

It helps organizations to:

- Update the sales prices and costs of multiple products in a single operation.
- Apply different price adjustment methods according to business requirements.
- Review proposed changes before they take effect.
- Track the outcome of each product-level update.
- Maintain historical records of price and cost changes.
- Improve transparency and accountability when managing product prices.

## 3. Main Areas of the Workflow

### 3.1. Product Price Updates

Product Price Updates is the main area for creating and managing price update batches.

A price update batch defines the scope of the update, the products involved, the reason for the change, and the rules used to calculate new sales prices and costs.

It supports a controlled process in which users can prepare an update, preview its results, apply the changes, or cancel the batch.

### 3.2. Product Price History

Product Price History provides a record of individual product-level results generated by price update batches.

It allows users to review the previous and new sales prices and costs, the differences between them, the related update batch, and the processing status.

This area supports traceability by connecting individual product price changes to the batch that generated them.

## 4. Key Capabilities

The workflow provides the following capabilities:

- **Bulk Price Updates:** Prepare price changes for multiple products through a single update batch.
- **Flexible Price Adjustment:** Configure sales price and cost changes using supported update methods and values.
- **Rounding Configuration:** Specify how calculated prices should be rounded.
- **Preview Before Application:** Review calculated product-level changes before applying them.
- **Controlled Application:** Apply a reviewed update batch and record its processing results.
- **Cancellation:** Cancel an update batch when it should not proceed.
- **Processing Summary:** Review the total number of product lines and the number of applied, skipped, and failed lines.
- **Price History Tracking:** Maintain product-level records of previous and updated sales prices and costs.
- **Auditability:** Identify the related update batch and available processing information for individual changes.

## 5. Benefits

- Reduces the effort required to update prices across multiple products.
- Helps users review changes before applying them.
- Provides better control over bulk price adjustments.
- Improves visibility into the results of price update operations.
- Supports the review and tracking of historical product price changes.
- Helps organizations maintain more consistent product pricing records.
