# Stock Issue Voucher - Feature Overview

## 1. Overview

A Stock Issue Voucher (SIV) is a document used to formally request, authorize, and record the issuance of inventory items from a store to a department, employee, project, or another authorized destination.

It provides a controlled process for managing internal stock consumption and distribution while maintaining accountability for the materials issued.

## 2. Purpose

The Stock Issue Voucher helps organizations:

- Control and authorize internal stock requests.
- Track requested, approved, and issued quantities.
- Verify stock availability before issuance.
- Associate stock consumption with departments, projects, or cost centers.
- Maintain records of stock movements and responsible personnel.
- Support inventory tracking and accounting.

## 3. Main Process

A typical Stock Issue Voucher process involves:

1. **Request Creation:** The requester identifies the required products, quantities, receiving party, destination, and responsible cost center.
2. **Submission:** The request is submitted for review and approval.
3. **Approval:** An authorized person reviews the request and approves or rejects it.
4. **Stock Availability:** The system checks whether the requested quantities are available.
5. **Stock Reservation:** Available quantities may be reserved to fulfill the approved request.
6. **Stock Issuance:** The storekeeper completes the issuance and records the actual quantities transferred.
7. **Record Keeping:** The completed voucher and its related inventory and accounting records are retained for tracking and reporting.

## 4. Key Information

A Stock Issue Voucher generally contains:

- Requester and receiver information.
- Department and cost center.
- Request date and reference number.
- Source store and destination.
- Requested products and quantities.
- Approved and issued quantities.
- Stock availability and reservation information.
- Approval and issuance details.
- Related inventory movements and accounting records.

## 5. Expected Benefits

- Improves control over internal stock distribution.
- Establishes accountability for stock requests and issuance.
- Reduces unauthorized or undocumented stock consumption.
- Provides traceability between requests and inventory movements.
- Supports departmental cost tracking and inventory reporting.
