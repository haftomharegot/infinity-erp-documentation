# Feature Overview - Replenishment

## 1. Overview

Replenishment is an inventory management workflow that helps organizations maintain appropriate stock levels by identifying products that may need replenishment and determining the quantities required to replenish them.

It uses predefined replenishment rules to monitor stock levels and support the process of requesting or ordering additional products when necessary.

These rules can consider factors such as minimum and maximum stock quantities, replenishment routes, procurement methods, and ordering quantities.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of Replenishment is to help organizations maintain sufficient stock to meet operational requirements while avoiding unnecessary inventory accumulation.

It helps organizations to:

- Monitor product stock levels and identify replenishment requirements.
- Define minimum and maximum stock quantities.
- Determine quantities required for replenishment.
- Support the procurement of products when stock needs to be replenished.
- Configure replenishment rules according to product and warehouse requirements.
- Manage replenishment activities through manual or automated processes.
- Temporarily postpone replenishment when immediate action is not required.

## 3. Main Areas of the Workflow

### 3.1. Replenishment Rules

Replenishment rules define how stock levels should be monitored and when replenishment should be considered.

A rule can be configured for a particular product and stock location, with parameters such as minimum quantity, maximum quantity, replenishment trigger, and ordering quantity.

These settings help determine when replenishment is required and how much stock should be requested.

### 3.2. Replenishment Monitoring

The replenishment monitoring area provides an overview of products and their stock conditions.

It allows users to review current stock, forecasted quantities, replenishment thresholds, and quantities indicated for ordering.

This helps users identify products that require attention and take appropriate replenishment actions.

### 3.3. Replenishment Actions

The workflow supports actions for initiating replenishment, managing automated replenishment, and postponing replenishment when necessary.

These actions help users manage replenishment requirements according to operational and procurement needs.

## 4. Key Capabilities

- **Stock Monitoring:** Review current and forecasted stock quantities.
- **Replenishment Rules:** Configure stock thresholds and replenishment parameters.
- **Quantity Planning:** Define minimum, maximum, and ordering quantity requirements.
- **Procurement Support:** Initiate replenishment through the available procurement processes.
- **Replenishment Automation:** Support automated replenishment based on configured rules.
- **Snoozing:** Temporarily postpone replenishment activities.
- **Multiple Configurations:** Support different replenishment configurations for products, locations, and warehouses.
- **Replenishment Visibility:** Review replenishment requirements through list and card-based views.

## 5. Benefits

- Helps maintain appropriate inventory levels.
- Reduces the likelihood of stock shortages.
- Supports better planning of purchasing requirements.
- Reduces the effort involved in monitoring stock manually.
- Provides greater control over replenishment quantities and conditions.
- Helps organizations manage replenishment activities more consistently.
