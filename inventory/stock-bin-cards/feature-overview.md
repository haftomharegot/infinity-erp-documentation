# Stock Bin Cards – Feature Overview

## 1. Introduction

A Stock Bin Card is an inventory record used to track the movement and balance of a particular product within a specific storage location over a defined period. It provides a chronological history of stock received, issued, and remaining in that location.

The bin card helps inventory personnel monitor stock levels, trace individual stock movements, and maintain an understandable record of how the quantity of a product changes over time.

## 2. Purpose

The primary purpose of Stock Bin Cards is to provide a clear and traceable record of inventory movements at the storage-location level.

It helps organizations to:

- Monitor the quantity of a product available in a particular storage location.
- Track stock receipts and issues over a selected period.
- Understand how stock quantities change over time.
- Verify the remaining quantity of a product in a storage location.
- Support physical stock verification and inventory reconciliation.
- Maintain a historical record for inventory monitoring and auditing.

## 3. Main Components

### 3.1 Card Identification

Identifies the product and storage location covered by the bin card. It establishes the scope of the stock movement record and may also include the associated company.

### 3.2 Reporting Period

Defines the period covered by the bin card through a start date and an end date. This determines which stock movements are included in the report.

### 3.3 Balance Summary

Provides an overview of the stock quantity at the beginning and end of the reporting period.

- **Opening Balance:** Quantity available at the beginning of the reporting period.
- **Closing Balance:** Quantity remaining at the end of the reporting period.

### 3.4 Stock Movement History

Displays the individual transactions that affect the product's quantity within the selected storage location. Each movement provides information such as:

- Movement date.
- Transaction reference.
- Related inventory operation.
- Source location.
- Destination location.
- Received quantity.
- Issued quantity.
- Running balance.
- Unit of measure.

### 3.5 Bin Card Generation

Generates the stock movement record and calculates the corresponding balances based on the selected product, storage location, and reporting period.

## 4. General Workflow

A typical Stock Bin Card workflow involves:

1. Selecting the product and storage location to be monitored.
2. Defining the reporting period.
3. Generating the bin card.
4. Retrieving the relevant stock movements.
5. Calculating the opening balance, running balances, and closing balance.
6. Reviewing the chronological movement history and stock quantities.

## 5. Expected Benefits

- **Stock Visibility:** Provides a clear view of stock quantities at individual storage locations.
- **Movement Traceability:** Makes it easier to identify when, how, and through which operations stock was received or issued.
- **Inventory Reconciliation:** Helps compare recorded stock balances with physical stock quantities.
- **Operational Monitoring:** Supports inventory personnel in monitoring stock movement and availability.
- **Historical Reference:** Provides a structured record of stock movement for reporting and auditing purposes.
