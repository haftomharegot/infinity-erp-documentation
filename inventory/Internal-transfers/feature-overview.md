# Internal Transfers – Feature Overview

## 1. Overview

Internal Transfers is an inventory workflow used to move products between locations within an organization. It supports the movement of stock between warehouses, storage areas, and other internal locations while maintaining records of the movement.

Unlike receipts and deliveries, which generally represent stock entering or leaving the organization's inventory, internal transfers concern movements between internal locations.

## 2. Purpose and Business Value

The Internal Transfers workflow helps organizations:

- Move products between warehouses and storage locations.
- Maintain accurate stock quantities across different locations.
- Record the source and destination of each movement.
- Track the progress and completion of internal stock movements.
- Maintain a history of transfers for inventory monitoring and accountability.

## 3. Main Areas and Capabilities

### 3.1 Transfer Creation

Users can create an internal transfer by specifying the relevant transfer details, including the source location, destination location, operation type, scheduled date, and products to be moved.

### 3.2 Transfer Management

Transfers can be reviewed and managed through their lifecycle. This includes monitoring their status, reviewing transfer information, and performing available operational actions.

### 3.3 Product Movement Details

Each transfer contains operation lines that identify the products being moved, their quantities, units of measure, and relevant movement information.

### 3.4 Transfer Validation and Completion

The workflow supports different transfer stages, allowing users to prepare, process, validate, and complete stock movements according to the organization's operational process.

### 3.5 Returns

The workflow can support the return of products associated with a previous transfer. A return creates a related inventory transfer record that can be reviewed and processed separately.

### 3.6 Detailed Operations and Allocation Information

Additional operational views can provide information about product movement details and the allocation or reception of products associated with a transfer.

## 4. Relationship with Other Business Functions

Internal Transfers is connected to other inventory operations because stock received, delivered, transferred, or processed through quality control and storage activities may move between different inventory locations.

It can also be associated with other business documents where a stock movement is created as part of a wider business process.

Maintaining clear links between these operations helps organizations follow the movement of products throughout their inventory operations.
